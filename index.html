📞 Official Fraud Hotlines

Call any of these official Ghanaian authorities to report fraud and cybercrime directly.

  • 🚓 Police CID (Cybercrime Unit) 18555
  • 🚓 Ghana Police Emergency 191 / 192
  • 🏦 MTN MoMo Fraud Team 100
  • 🏦 MTN Customer Care 0552300000
  • 🏛️ EOCO (Economic & Organised Crime) 0302 966 636
  • 📡 NCA (National Communications Authority) 0302 774 321
  • 🔒 CSA (Cyber Security Authority) 292
  • ⚠️ Report Scam SMS Send to 400

For emergencies, always call 191 first.
